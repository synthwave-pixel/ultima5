# The box painting

Scans of Denis Loubet's painting for the box of Ultima V (Origin Systems,
1988), for the key art (`docs/key-art-brief.md`, `web/tools/key-art.mjs`).
The painting remains its owners'.

| File                         | Size       | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `box-front-crop-800x520.png` | 800 × 520  | The box's front, its top half: the finest scan of the Shadowlords and the knight, framed tightly (it cuts off the fallen friend, whom the key art keeps). The box's red edges down each side and "By Lord British" at the top right are in it. |
| `cover-clean-528x704.png`    | 528 × 704  | The whole painting with no frame; the box's title lettered over its top.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `box-front-583x800.png`      | 583 × 800  | The whole box front: frame, title, "By Lord British", the platform label.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `cover-fmtowns-556x800.png`  | 556 × 800  | The FM Towns release's cover: the same painting between Japanese bands.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `cover-hires-900x1301.png`   | 900 × 1301 | The finest scan and the most of the painting (both sides, the foot), the box's title lettered over its top; colours warmer than the box scans', and sharpened. The source of the key art.                                                      |
