Dungeon map cells

Every cell value in the dungeons' levels (DUNGEON.DAT), with where it is found and how often, then the values play makes: what the map takes each for, how each map draws it, the first-person view with it one step ahead, and what Look says (and a sign's words). Each is the game's own drawing, cut out of a real screen: on the maps the cell in the middle of a level of rock, a passage on each side of it (so the PC (1988) maps' arms show); the Modern small map is the three cells a side round the party, who stands west of the cell. Drawn in the light of the dungeon chosen, or of the first it is found in.
