The runes

A dungeon sign carved into its dungeon's wall ahead, its runes giving way to their English in a wave, as the game reads it, and the same sign printed in the log; or, from the other groups, runes printed in the log as the game prints them there - a key goes on where it waits for one, and finishes the reading at once, as in play. Modern reads printed runes into English; PC (1988) keeps them runes. The wall's choices (carve to plate) are for dungeon signs alone.
