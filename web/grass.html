Grass

Modern PC's grass is two colours: the ground between the blades (the original's black, toned) and the blades. Every tile with grass in it and everything standing on it is drawn again as you move the sliders - the roads, the edges and the shores take it from the grass too. Below the world the land is drawn darker and colder, the grass with it. The colours are kept in the page's address, so it can be sent as it is.
