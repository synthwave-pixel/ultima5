Test pages

  • Tiles Every tile in the Original and Standard sets side by side, animated; each tile's versions, and real stretches of the map drawn as the game draws them. Above them, the game at any place, battle arena or dungeon room, with Next. Only: StandardPC EGA
  • Tile deltas The tiles Modern PC still draws in the Standard art, each beside the original and the original as Modern PC would draw it - whole, or lifted onto its ground - with a choice for each, kept here and copied out as a list.
  • Grass Modern PC's grass: the ground between its blades and the blades set by hue, saturation and value, and every tile with grass in it, what stands on it and stretches of Britannia drawn again as they change.
  • Appearance The Avatar's figures with their regions (skin, hair, main clothes, trim) marked, every skin, hair colour and hue on each, and the Avatar walking as chosen.
  • Sound effects Every sound the game makes, with what it is, when and how often it plays, where in the code, and the Standard set's voice; play each in either set.
  • Pictures Every picture in the game's picture files, Original and Standard side by side.
  • Dungeon corridor The first-person dungeon view for every kind of cell, as the game composes it, Original and Standard side by side.
  • Dungeon map cells Every cell value in the dungeons as each dungeon map draws it, and what Look says of it.
  • The runes Every dungeon sign carved on its wall and read into English, and printed in the log; carve style and face to compare. And every other place runes are printed - signs looked at, words spoken in runes, the Codex, a position, ALAKAZAM, the ending - printed in the log, in either look.

The game

The starts below work in a development build only; elsewhere they open the game as usual.

  • Play The game from its title screen.
  • Continue, skipping the title The saved game, straight in (a new one where there is no save).
  • New game, skipping the title A fresh party at the start, whatever is saved. Its first autosave replaces the save.
  • Start at a town or castle At the place's south gate.
  • Start in a dungeon On its first level, at the entrance; or from below, on its last level, as from the Underworld.
  • Start in the Underworld Beneath a dungeon's entrance, where its lowest ladder comes out; Doom's own door.
  • The ending The ending's pictures, one after another.
