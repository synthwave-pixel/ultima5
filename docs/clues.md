**This is one long spoiler.** It lists all 106 lines the journal counts as clues, who says them, and what to say to hear them.

The journal's *Places* page shows a count such as Yew 5/10. The game builds that count by looking for quest words in everything townsfolk can say: Mantra, Word of Power, Shard, Shadowlord, Codex, Crown, Sceptre, Amulet, Sandalwood, Doom, Underworld, Lord British, Resistance, Password, Moonstone, Flame, Glass Sword, Grapple, Shrine, Harpsichord, Phrase and the Shadowlords' names. So some counted lines are small talk, and some vital lines aren't counted (see [What the journal doesn't count](#what-the-journal-doesnt-count)).

**★** marks a line that really moves the quest on. The rest are colour, though you still need them for a full count.

## How to read a route

- **Ask** means say that word at "Your interest?". The game usually reads only the first four letters, so CONVICTED and CONV are the same.
- **answer** means say it when they've just asked you a question.
- With a controller, a word appears on the Say list once you've heard it. If it isn't there yet, use Add word or go and hear it first.
- **Answer any opening question first.** Some people greet you with a question, and it takes your next reply.
- **Give your real name when asked.** "What is thy name?" can come mid-conversation, and it takes your next reply. A false name gets "If you say so...", and with some people, such as Sutek and Landon, the talk ends there.
- "Needs" lists words the speaker doesn't lead you to on their own, and where to get them.

## The chains

Most clues only take a conversation. These are the ones that send you elsewhere first.

### 1. The Resistance and its password, DAWN
1. **Terrance, Britain:** ask HARDER, then SHADOWLORDS. He mentions the Resistance.
2. **Chamfort, Yew:** ask RESISTANCE → answer TERRANCE → Yes. He tells you to see Landon, and that the password is **DAWN**.
3. **Chamfort:** ask LANDON, then LOCATION or LEADER, to learn about the secret passage through his fireplace.
4. DAWN then opens **Landon** (Yew), **Felespar** (Yew, Word of Power for Wrong), **Joshua** (North Britanny), **Zachariah** (Moonglow, sends you to Goeth) and **Hassad** (Blackthorn's dungeon, Word of Power for Hythloth).

### 2. The Mantra of Justice
1. **Jeremy, Yew:** ask 500, GOLD or CROWNS → Yes, paying 30 gold → INFORMATION. He says Chamfort knows a mantra.
2. **Chamfort:** ask MANTRA → answer JEREMY. The mantra is **BEH**.

### 3. Infiltrating the Oppression
1. **Flain, Skara Brae:** answer OPPRESSION → JUDGE or DRYDEN → Yes → name a Great Council member. The password is **Impera**.
2. **Saduj, Lord British's castle:** ask WATERING → answer anything twice → Yes → IMPERA → No. He reveals his mission, and ask BLACKTHORN for the sandalwood box.
3. **Elistaria, Windemere:** say IMPERA for the badge. This isn't a counted clue.

### 4. The Word of Power for Covetous
1. **Annon, Britain:** ask BLACKTHORN at karma 75 or better → Yes. He gives you **VILIS** and points to a sailmaker's child.
2. **Rew, Minoc:** ask MOTHER. Her mother is Fiona.
3. **Fiona, Minoc:** follow the route under Minoc below for **AVIDUS**.

### 5. Greymarch and the Sceptre
**Froed**, hiding in Skara Brae, is Greymarch's son. **Greymarch**, in Yew's jail, answers news of him by telling you about the Sceptre and Sir Simon.

### 6. The Shards
- **Joshua** (after DAWN) points to **Sutek**, who explains how to destroy Shards and Shadowlords.
- **Lady Janell** sends you to the sisters in **Cove** (Falsehood).
- **Lord Michael** (ask SHADOWLORDS, not counted) sends you to **Sin'Vraal** (Hatred).
- **Gardner**, at Serpent's Hold, gives the Shard of Cowardice directly.

### 7. The harpsichord
**Tim**, at Empath Abbey, says Sir Kenneth is travelling. **Lord Kenneth**, at Greyhaven, teaches the phrase.

### 8. The Spirituality shrine
**Saul**, Skara Brae → **Kindor**, at the healer's near 6 P.M. → **Lady Janell**, the Lycaeum. Tell **Glinkie** in Paws too.

No townsperson says the word for Doom. The Codex gives it, along with directions, when it answers the eighth quest. To use it, reach the isle at the heart of the Underworld from Shame's exit and cross the peaks with Blink (In Por). Wear Lord British's Amulet to walk through the darkness, then yell the word next to the mouth at the centre. The gate attacks you and keeps you out until all three Shadowlords are destroyed.

The Codex answers only the first active quest, in shrine order, so return to that shrine before seeking the next answer.
